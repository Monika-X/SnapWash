The Essential Guide to Silk Preservation

Oct 12, 2026 Master Cleaner team

Silk is an investment in elegance. Its natural protein fibers provide unparalleled drape and luster, but they also require meticulous care to prevent degradation over time. At SnapWash, we treat silk with the reverence it deserves.

1. The Danger of DIY

While many labels state "hand wash," doing so with standard household detergents strips the fibers of their natural oils. This leads to brittleness and a loss of the signature silk sheen.

2. Our Professional Protocol

We utilize a highly specialized, low-moisture dry cleaning process specifically calibrated for delicate proteins. Our solvents are pH-neutral and incredibly gentle, ensuring that structural integrity remains completely intact.

3. Storage Best Practices

Always store silk in a cool, dark environment. Use breathable cotton garment bags rather than plastic to prevent moisture accumulation, which can lead to fiber-destroying mildew.
