Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 Last reviewed September 2026

Your garments are entrusted to us — and so is your data. At SnapWash, we treat privacy with the same exacting care we apply to every fiber we handle.

01

Introduction

At SnapWash, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website, regardless of where you visit it from.

02

Data We Collect

We may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data about you, including:

  • Identity data — name, title, and membership details
  • Contact data — billing address, email, and telephone
  • Financial data — payment details and invoice history
  • Transaction data — order history and service preferences
03

How We Use Your Data

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data to perform the contract we are about to enter into or have entered into with you, such as processing your laundry orders, scheduling pickups, and delivering your garments.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties. Ever.

04

Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. Access is limited to team members who require it to deliver our services, and all data is stored on encrypted systems.

05

Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to object to certain processing activities. To exercise any of these rights, simply contact our privacy desk — we will respond within 30 days.
