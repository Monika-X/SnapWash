Our Heritage

Born from a Passion for Textiles.

SnapWash was founded on a simple premise: your garments are investments that deserve artisan-level care. We recognized a gap in the market for a service that truly understood the nuances of luxury fabrics, while offering the seamless convenience demanded by modern life.

Our facilities employ cutting-edge eco-friendly technology, overseen by master cleaners with decades of combined experience. We don't just clean clothes; we restore and preserve their original elegance.

Our Journey

Milestones of Craft

2022 — THE BEGINNING

SnapWash opens as a single atelier in Metropolis with two master technicians and a borrowed van.

2023 — THE ATELIER NETWORK

Three neighborhood boutiques open, each with dedicated couture and restoration benches.

2024 — ZERO-WASTE STANDARD

We become the first garment care house in the region to certify a fully biodegradable solvent cycle.

2025 — THE PRIVATE LIST

Our membership program launches with priority scheduling, wardrobe consultations, and seasonal care.

2026 — 15,000 GARMENTS RESTORED

A milestone of trust. Every one of them inspected, cleaned, pressed, and delivered with care.
