Frequently Asked Questions

What is your pickup and delivery schedule?

Our concierge offers daily doorstep pickup and delivery in the greater Metropolis area, including early morning and evening windows. Select your preferred slot when booking through your dashboard and we will confirm within minutes.

How does your pricing work?

Wash & Fold is priced per pound, while Dry Cleaning is priced per item based on garment type. Couture and specialty pieces receive a custom quote after inspection. All prices are confirmed transparently before processing.

How long does a typical order take?

Standard turnaround is 48 hours from pickup to delivery. Express service is available in as little as 24 hours, and same-day service can be arranged for select orders before 9 AM.

What care methods do you use for delicate fabrics?

Delicates are handled with specialized silicone-based solvents and hand-finishing techniques. Every garment is assessed by a master technician before processing, and care labels are always respected.

Do you offer a satisfaction guarantee?

Absolutely. If a garment does not meet our exacting standards, we will re-clean it free of charge or refund the service in full. Your wardrobe's wellbeing is our first priority.

How do I become a member?

Membership is complimentary for all clients. Join through the signup page to unlock priority scheduling, complimentary wardrobe consultations, and specialized seasonal care.

Prefer to Talk in Person?

Visit our Metropolis atelier for a complimentary fabric consultation, or book a pickup from the comfort of your home.
