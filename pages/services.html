Exquisite Services

Tailored care protocols for every fiber in your wardrobe.

Premium Wash & Fold

Our everyday luxury service. We sort colors perfectly, wash at optimal temperatures using plant-based detergents, and fold with geometric precision. Your items are returned in custom eco-totes, smelling faintly of clean linen and fresh air.

Executive Dry Cleaning

The ultimate preservation for your tailored suits, silk blouses, and formal wear. We utilize a gentle, specialized silicone-based solvent that lifts stains without degrading fibers, ensuring your garments maintain their structural integrity and vibrant colors.

Artisan Steam Press

Crisp, museum-quality pressing for dress shirts, trousers, and formal wear. Our artisans use calibrated steam pressure to set sharp creases and soften stubborn wrinkles without crushing fabric fibers — finished on premium wooden hangers and delivered ready to wear.

Couture Care & Restoration

For bespoke tailoring, couture gowns, leather, and suede — a white-glove program of stain archaeology, invisible mending, and archival-grade preservation. Each piece is assessed by our master restorer, quoted transparently, and handled with documentation of every treatment step.
