Dry Cleaning

Executive Dry Cleaning

The ultimate preservation for your tailored suits, silk blouses, and formal wear. We utilize a gentle, specialized silicone-based solvent that lifts stains without degrading fibers, ensuring your garments maintain their structural integrity and vibrant colors.

  • Silicone-based solvent care

Artisan Steam Press

Crisp, museum-quality pressing for dress shirts, trousers, and formal wear. Our artisans use calibrated steam pressure to set sharp creases and soften stubborn wrinkles without crushing fabric fibers — finished on premium wooden hangers and delivered ready to wear.

  • Calibrated steam, never crushed
  • Premium wooden hangers included
  • Ready-to-wear, same-day option
